A2 · Research

When AI answers for us, do we still think?

My research tests how students who hand their thinking to generative AI end up reasoning, and names a risk I call the Artificial Illusion of Knowledge (AIK): the feeling of understanding something that the machine, not the mind, has worked out. It rests on Dual-Process Theory and Cognitive Load Theory, and is measured with PLS-SEM.

183

management students analysed in the FEBIC 2026 study, screened from 263 responses under pre-set rules.

β0.573

self-regulated learning to reasoning depth. Cognitive outsourcing ran the other way (β = −0.189).

R²0.499

variance in scientific reasoning depth explained in the Dean Research study (n = 100, p < 0.001).
